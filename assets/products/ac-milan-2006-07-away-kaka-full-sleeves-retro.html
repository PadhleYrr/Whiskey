      Standard delivery takes 4–6 working days across India. However, depending on your location, it may take7–8 days for remote areas or during high-demand periods.

      What if I receive a damaged or wrong product ?

      100% Free Replacement: We've got you covered if your jersey arrives incorrect or damaged!

      Mandatory Unboxing Video: To claim your replacement, you must record a continuous, 360-degree video the exact moment your parcel arrives.

      No Edits Allowed: The video must show the shipping label clearly and be filmed in a single take (absolutely no cuts, trims, or edits).
